Controls

Rotate
Left-click and drag
Pan
Right-click and drag, or Shift + drag, or Shift + arrow keys
Zoom
Mouse wheel, pinch, Zoom +/- or the + / - keys
Select
Click a structure (or a row in the list)
Box select
Hold Ctrl and drag with the left mouse; add Shift to add to the current selection
Multi-select
Ctrl or Shift + click several parts, then press Isolate: the camera jumps to them
Systems
Tick several systems to view them together
Name
Hover over a structure
Touch
One finger rotates; two fingers zoom and pan
X-ray
Select a part and drag the X-ray slider next to Systems: 0% shows everything, 100% hides everything except the selection
Section
Open Section, pick a plane and drag the slider to cut through the body
